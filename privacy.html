Privacy Policy

Moonlit Blade 3D does not collect, share or sell personal information.

Your game progress (coins, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your own device, in your browser or app storage. It is never sent to us.

The game loads the open-source three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, which may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

The game has no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
